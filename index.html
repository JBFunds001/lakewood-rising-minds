Our mission

Every young person deserves someone in their corner. We show up with mentoring, learning and real chances to lead, so Lakewood's youth can rise to who they're meant to be.

Read our story

What we do

Three programs, one goal: help every young person find their footing and their voice.

01

Mentorship

One-on-one time with a caring adult who helps them set goals, push through setbacks and believe in what they can do.
02

Learning support

Tutoring and study help at their own pace, turning "I can't" into "I've got this," one assignment at a time.
03

Leadership & community

Youth-led projects where they serve their neighborhood and discover the change they're able to make.
